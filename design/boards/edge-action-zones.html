Board 80 · Edge action zones: drag a pad to an edge to act on it

Batch item 12, Scott’s idea: once a held pad lifts, it can go to another slot (move, as now) or to one of the screen’s edges, each an action. Directions kept from the flick idea: bottom = Clear, right = Edit, left = Duplicate; the top edge is left free (the header lives there; a fourth action could go there later). iPhone landscape at 1:1, a right hand; track 3’s pad A 08 mid-drag, hovering the right edge (Edit) in every tile, so you can compare the idle and the hot zone. Five styles.

  • Behaviour, same for all five: the zones arrive as the pad lifts (they slide or fade in from their edge, nothing pops) and leave on drop. A zone lights when the pad’s centre is within ~48 pt of its edge; the lifted pad tilts toward it. Dropping on Clear clears with the Undo strip (item 13), no confirm. Dropping anywhere else that isn’t a slot puts the pad back.
  • Colour: Edit and Duplicate use the track’s colour (they act on the track’s pad); Clear uses red only while hot, grey at rest, as the craft bar keeps red for destruction.
  • Room: the zones sit over the outer pads while dragging, so the left zone covers the track heads, which can’t be drop targets anyway; the bottom zone covers the scenes row.
  • VoiceOver and keyboards keep the hold menu, which still has every action.
