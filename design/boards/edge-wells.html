Board 81 · Dashed wells: placement and resting fills

Iterations on board 80’s style 4. Same scene in each: track 3’s pad A 08 lifted and dragged over the right well (Edit), so the right well shows the hot state and the other two the resting one. Bottom Clear, right Edit, left Duplicate (actions open below). Two placements (inset 12 pt, or attached flush to the edge, square on the edge side, rounded inside) × resting fills (dark glass, the track’s muted colour as glass, grey frost). Glass = the fill at partial opacity with a 12 pt blur of the pads behind it.

  • Hot state, all tiles: the well goes solid track colour, its dashes turn solid, the label goes dark, and the lifted pad tilts toward it. Clear’s hot state is red (only while hot).
  • Attached wells read as part of the screen’s frame, so they look less like more slots; they also run into the rounded corners, so their ends stop short of them.
  • Muted track colour at rest ties the wells to the pad being dragged (it changes with the track), but puts colour on three edges at once; dark glass is calmer and lets the hot one stand out more.
