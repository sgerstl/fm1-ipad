Board 77 · The Mix pop-up: no title, tabs beside the close key, the FM-1’s own pictures

iPhone 17 Pro in landscape, 874 × 402 pt, at 1:1. GLO on the FM-1 (or the song-name menu) opens the pop-up. Decision 31: no “Mix” title; the five tabs sit in line with the close key, and the room goes to the picture. The pictures here are the FM-1’s own drawings, rendered by Skiff 2.5.1’s drawing code (its render harness, 240 × 240 screen, the graph panel cropped), shown at the FM-1’s proportions. Drawn in the global greys and white (Scott, 2026-11-14: GLO pages are shared, so no track colour); FX › DELAY and FX › REVERB, the per-track send pages, keep the track’s colour. The build redraws them as vectors from the same formulas: the FM-1’s shapes, proportions and stepped finish, in grey.

What moves (each pair is the same page at two settings, from the FM-1)

What the build has to change, and one decision

  • Colour, decided: greys and white for the GLO pages; the track colour only on the per-track FX pages. The redraw keeps the reverb’s three tones apart in grey (white reflections, a light body, a dark DAMP band). Background: On 2026-11-06 the iPad drew every GLO picture grey (“white is global”). The FM-1 draws DELAY, REVERB, CHORUS and GLOBAL in the selected track’s colour. The grey is the main reason the reverb doesn’t look like the FM-1’s: its bright body under a dark DAMP band, beside white reflections, loses its contrast. Proposal: pictures follow the FM-1 (track colour); the tabs, dials and the sheet itself stay neutral, so the global rule still holds for the controls.
  • Proportions: the FM-1’s graph panel is 1.7 : 1. The iPad stretched it into a 128 pt card two to a row; here it keeps 1.7 : 1 at about 476 × 280 pt.
  • Finish: square bar corners, 1 px stepped tails, no gradients or opacity fades the FM-1 doesn’t draw.
  • Android: today it draws no GLO pictures at all (MixPage “no graph”); CHORUS and GLOBAL have no drawing. FX › DELAY also assumes the bus MIX is 127 where the FM-1 uses GLO › DELAY’s MIX (default 90), so its first echo is ~40 % too tall.
  • MIXER keeps its levels and pans (not shown).
  • Theme: CRYPT, NEON and QUARK draw these pages completely differently. Which theme is Scott’s FM-1 on (MENU › THEME)? If it isn’t SKIFF, the phone should match that theme or say it shows SKIFF’s.
