The hold menu: a circle under the finger, lines out to roomy items

Press and hold a pad or a track head (about 0.35 s). The circle grows under your finger, the lines run out, the items settle. Drag onto an item and let go to pick it, or let go first and tap. Tap outside, or press Escape, to close. Clear asks again in place.

LUPE TEST ⌄PadsEditTimeline
  1. The circle is the track's colour at about 45 % over the page (the "muted" colour), 64 dp at rest, so it shows round the finger. It grows from nothing past its size (×1.3) and springs back with one small undershoot. The Spring slider sets how much it overshoots; 0 is a plain ease-out. A white dot appears in its centre once it settles: the anchor the lines leave from.
  2. The lines run from the circle's edge to each item, 1.5 dp in the track's colour, drawing out in turn (30 ms apart), each ending in a small node at the item's edge. The lit item's line goes white.
  3. The items are stacked one above another, 56 dp apart, but each sits where that height meets a circle of 120 dp around the finger, so the stack bows outwards with the circle. Each is a 48 dp tall rounded rectangle, at least 176 dp wide, with Barlow 16. Near the right edge the menu opens to the left. Items are clamped inside the screen.
  4. Picking: drag from the hold onto an item and let go (one gesture), or let go and tap. The item under the finger lights (lifted surface) with a tick of haptics on the phone. A destructive item asks again in place (DESIGN.md): it turns into a red-outlined button naming what goes, and ignores taps for 0.4 s.
  5. Leaving: items and lines fade (120 ms), the circle shrinks to nothing (exits faster than entrances). Reduce motion: everything fades in and out, nothing grows or travels.
  6. Usable first: every item is at least 48 dp tall with a clear label; the scrim dims the page so the menu reads. TalkBack gets the same items as a plain list (role menu); the drawing is decoration.
