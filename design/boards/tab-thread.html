Tab transitions: the track thread, beside a slower plain slide

Tap Pads, Edit and Timeline (or use ← →). Track 3 (Rave) is selected. The thread version keeps its yellow outline on screen while the pages slide, and the outline reshapes into whatever that tab shows of track 3.

LUPE TEST ⌄
  1. The thread: the selected track's 2 dp outline in its colour (DESIGN.md: the track's colour is the track's). It never leaves the screen during a tab change: it eases from its rectangle on the old tab to its rectangle on the new one while both pages slide underneath. Pads: the track's row (head to the last pad). Edit: the step row, the track's pattern. Timeline: the track's lane. Your eye stays on the track and the pages move round it.
  2. The pages slide in the tab's direction (Pads → Edit → Timeline goes left), the outgoing page slightly faster than the incoming. The outline moves on the same curve and lands with the incoming page, so nothing arrives late.
  3. Plain slide is the same page motion with no thread, at the duration you set: this is the "just slow it down" option.
  4. Reduce motion: no sliding; the pages cross-fade (150 ms) and the outline cross-fades between its two places.
  5. Default proposed: 380 ms (today's build is 280), ease-out, outgoing page ends at 85 % of the time.
