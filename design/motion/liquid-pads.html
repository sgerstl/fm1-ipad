Lupe liquid motion

The play colour behaves like liquid pressed between two sheets. It has a fixed amount, so it can only move: launching a pad pours that track's colour out of the old pad and into the new one, and muting squeezes it into the ring. Tap pads and Mute, drag across a full pad to push its bubbles, and pull the levers.
