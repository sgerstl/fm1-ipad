**Setup:** **Skiff-Lupe 2.5.1 take** on the FM-1 (`firmware/releases/Skiff-Lupe-2.5.1-take.fwsc`, SHA-256 and size in `Skiff-Lupe-2.5.1-take.md`, identity `FM-1_725`, INFO `56 01 07`; skiff-src branch `lupe-skiff-2.5.1-freetake`) · Lupe from branch `claude/freetake-2-3b5b1d` · USB cable, the FM-1's screen in view. **Back up first? Yes.** Installing writes the FM-1's flash, and checks 3–8 replace T1's pattern or undo it. Before installing: Lupe › Projects, save the music to the iPad, and let Install firmware make its own backup.
