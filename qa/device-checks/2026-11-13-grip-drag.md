# Device check: sizing a note from its grip (2026-11-13)

Scott: "Single finger panning keeps me from being able to change the size of the block. I can only slide it one step at a time."

Cause: the grid asked "is this finger on a note's grip?" on every move, against the note as it was then. One step of sizing moved the note's end away from where the finger landed, the answer became "no", and the one-finger pan took the touch. Fix: `TwoFingerScrollView` decides once, when the finger lands (`landedCanPan`).

On the iPad (and the phone's piano roll), Synth track, Steps:

1. Drag a note's right end several steps to the right in one motion. It grows all the way and the grid doesn't scroll.
2. Drag it back left several steps in one motion. It shrinks all the way.
3. Start just past a note's end (the empty cell after it) and drag right. It grows.
4. One finger anywhere else still pans, and two fingers still pan.
5. Hold (the ring fills), then drag a note. It moves. Hold on empty grid, then drag. It draws a box.
6. Tap still adds, picks and deletes.
