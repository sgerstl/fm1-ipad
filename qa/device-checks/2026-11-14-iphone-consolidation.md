# Device check: iPhone consolidation (2026-11-14, branch claude/iphone-consolidation-6090a2)

Simulator only so far (iPhone 17 Pro, `-demo -demo-skiff -demo-fw 2.5.1 -demo-link`). No step writes the FM-1's flash, so no backup is needed unless noted.

1. **Header.** Open Lupe. You should see the song name with ⌄ at the left, Pads · Edit · Timeline, then the launch word (Bar ⌄), the readout and round Record and Play at the right. Tap the song name: Projects, Mix, Settings, Play through the iPhone, the demos, Developer.
2. **Tab change.** Tap Pads → Edit → Timeline and back. Pages slide in the tab's direction (about 380 ms) and the selected track's coloured outline travels from its row to the grid to its lane, then fades. Tune in Settings › Labs › Tab change.
3. **Pads heads.** Tap a head: it selects only. Hold a head: the radial menu opens under the finger. Swipe down the MUTE column: every key you cross takes the first key's new state.
4. **Pads slots.** While playing, slots show ×N, the playing one n/N, the next one "next". Tap a pad while playing: its outline draws round as the launch point comes. Same on a MUTE key tapped while playing.
5. **Hold menu.** Hold a pad: circle, lines and choices; Clear asks again in red before it goes (back up first if you confirm a clear: it erases a pattern). Tune spring and speed in Labs.
6. **Sound panel.** Press LFO on the FM-1: the panel slides in from the right in the track's muted colour with four dials and the picture. Press LFO again: next page. HOME, the arrow or another tab closes it. On Edit, the grid still scrolls to step 16 under it.
7. **Edit.** The note names are a piano edge: white and black keys, C named. A kit track still shows its lanes. Press a key: it plays.
8. **Timeline.** Blocks across, − and + change the bars shown, tap a block picks it (white ring), hold opens its menu.
9. **Sheets.** Song name › Mix…: tabs beside ✕, no title. Hold a head › Select sound: the Sounds sheet rises. SAVE on the FM-1: Projects with the four slots. Settings has Show graphics.
10. **No FM-1.** Unplug: the card says Skiff 2.5.1.
