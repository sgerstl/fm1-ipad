# Device check: android FREE TAKE (claude/android-p4-sound-pages-7e2a23), 2026-11-14

Written by the session that built it (see `CLAUDE.md`, rule 2). Scott runs it on the Pixel with the FM-1; the branch merges only after it passes. **FREE TAKE on the phone: a phrase played on the FM-1 comes back through Skiff's FREE TAKE, and Lupe shows the picker** (decision 5, revised: the FM-1's keys never write from the phone). Board 71 (`design/boards/phone-portrait-2.html`).

**Firmware:** this needs Skiff-Lupe's **screen link v7** (TAKE 94, UNDO 95). In the iPad app that is the "take" build of 2.5.1 (`Skiff-Lupe-2.5.1-take.fwsc`, `LupeFirmware.skiff251Take`); stock 2.5.1 has FREE TAKE level 1 only (the take lands, no picker from here). Check on the iPad first that its picker works on this FM-1: the phone can't install firmware. Without v7 the phone shows the take's landing only, with "SAVE held, then OCT− undoes it on the FM-1" instead of an Undo key, and "Keep that" is replaced by "Press SHIFT + SAVE on the FM-1 to keep it".

**Setup:** FM-1 on the take build, USB to the Pixel · the build of this branch (merged with P1's core) · **writes allowed** for checks 2 to 6 (the debug screen's switch: `adb shell am start -n com.scottgerstl.lupe/.core.debug.LinkDebugActivity --ez allowWrites true`; the app restarts; turn it off after). Check 1 is read-only.
**Back up first?** Yes, for checks 4 and 5: Keep it writes the take into a track (a pattern, the length, the division and, unless another track has notes, the tempo). Back up with Lupe on the iPad (Projects), or SLOOP's editor (Song & sections › Save a backup). Undo (check 5) brings the track back, but the backup is the safe way.

**Not covered here** (needs the device, the FM-1 and TalkBack): the take build on the FM-1 itself; the layout at font scales above 130%; the strip's position over a step selected near the top of the editor (the strip covers the first row of the step grid until you let it go or keep it); the offer's 12 s timeout (`FreeTakeWatch.offerFor`) and the iPad's "land on the Timeline" after a keep: the phone has no Timeline yet, so after a keep you stay in the editor.

**Provisional calls (mine):** the strip sits on top of the editor rather than pushing it down (so nothing moves); the picker's choices scroll at split (board 71 shows the fade); the confirm for Keep it is the iPad's, in the same words; the notes grid is the iPad's (a 16th grid, bar lines strong), drawn at 84 dp at split and 150 dp at bottom full; Listen/Stop is `playing` from the transport.

**Checked here:** PhoneCore's area, intents and read-only refusal over FakeSkiff 2.5.1 with the v7 link (8 tests: offer, start, each choice, keep, undo, put back, the refusal, no-link); the fixtures `take-offer`, `take-picker`, `take-landed` are recordings; Kotlin tests for the decode, the fake's click-through and the intents; screenshots on an emulator with those fixtures (the picker and the strip rendered as boarded; an emulator system dialog covered part of them). The board (71) was rendered and read; the design gate: contrast and targets by construction (48 dp targets, the tokens), no automated tells check on Kotlin (`check-tells.sh` doesn't scan it).
