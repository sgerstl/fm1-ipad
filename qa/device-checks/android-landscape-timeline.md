# Device check: the landscape Timeline (claude/android-p4-sound-pages-7e2a23), 2026-11-14

Written by the session that built it (see `CLAUDE.md`, rule 2). Scott runs it on the Pixel 10 Pro with the FM-1; the branch merges only after it passes. **Decision 28: the phone is landscape, and on the Timeline time runs across.** This replaces the portrait Timeline (time down); nothing else on the screen changed here. No board yet: built to the brief, from board 46's Pads rows and the iPad's arrange lane.

**What it is:** `ui/timeline/TimelinePage.kt` (same name and signature as before, so the shell's call is unchanged), `TimelineView.kt` (zoom and pan arithmetic), and the drag arithmetic it already had (`TimelineDrag.kt`, direction-free). Six track rows with 72 dp heads, a bar ruler, blocks as bars of the track's colour.

**Setup:** the combined build with P2's landscape shell and this branch · FM-1 on Skiff 2.5.1 · **writes allowed** for checks 4 to 9 (`adb shell am start -n com.scottgerstl.lupe/.core.debug.LinkDebugActivity --ez allowWrites true`) · a project with a song on several tracks (a few blocks each, one REST). Without the FM-1, checks 1 to 9 run on the fake core (`--es core fake`, scenario `drums`).
**Back up first?** Yes, for checks 5 to 9: they change the song's order, passes and entries on the FM-1 (Lupe on the iPad › Projects, or SLOOP's editor › Song & sections › Save a backup).

**Not covered here** (needs the Pixel, the FM-1 or TalkBack): how the drags feel under a hand (injected swipes on an emulator worked: the grip set 4 to 6 bars, a body drag swapped two blocks, both kept at release); animation hitches; the row heights lining up with P2's Pads rows once it sits in the landscape shell (it fills what it is given: six rows share the height under a 32 dp ruler); font scales above 130 %; the Pixel 7's 914 × 411.

**Decision 29 (added 2026-11-14):** the selected track's row is outlined in the track's colour, as Pads does it; a selected block keeps its white ring (the block is this screen's selection, not the track). Built and compiled; not seen on screen.

**The hold menu (added 2026-11-14, P2's `HoldMenu.kt`, Scott's approved radial menu):** the block menu and the empty-lane menu use it. Its choices are pick-and-go, so **PASSES is a list of common values there, not a slide** (my call; P2 suggested keeping the old menu for a slide row: say if you want that back). Seen on an emulator in P2's shell: the menu opening on a block, and a tap and the grip drag still working beside it. Not seen: picking by sliding, the second sets, the confirm.

**Provisional calls (mine, no board):**
1. The heads are 72 dp: a 24 dp number chip on the track's colour and the name in two lines of 11 sp. No Mute key (Pads has it).
2. The ruler row is 32 dp. The zoom keys − + sit over the heads' column and the page keys ‹ › at the ruler's right end; they are 34 × 26 dp keys in 32 dp targets (above WCAG's 24 dp, under the 44 dp aim: there is no height for more). They exist because a pinch and a pan need a single-tap way in (WCAG 2.5.1, 2.5.7).
3. The Stop strip sits on the right of the ruler (it replaces the page keys while playing), so no lane is covered. Portrait had it across the foot.
4. Zoom is 8 to 16 bars across, 12 to begin with; the pan ends four bars past the song.
5. While playing the view pages when the playhead passes nine tenths of the lanes (to a tenth in from the left), and waits 3 s after a finger moved it.
6. Dragging a block within 36 dp of either end of the lanes pans the song 10 dp a move.
7. The playing block's mark is still the bar along its top edge, and "next" the thin outline, as portrait.
8. A block's number rides the lanes' left edge when the block starts before it (added after the emulator look; that one fix was built and tested by the unit tests' build only, not seen on screen).

**Checked here:** 77 Kotlin unit tests pass (new `TimelineViewTest`: zoom limits, pan ends, the pinch's focus, ruler numbering, playhead following, edge pan, and a drag read at its end); the APK builds. On an emulator in a throwaway landscape host (not committed): the layout at 915 × 412, select, the grip drag, the reorder drag, the hold menu, the playing strip, + and ›. Not on a device, not with TalkBack. Design pre-flight: structure first (rows as Pads), grayscale hierarchy holds (blocks by value, the selection by a white ring), tokens only (Tok, LupeType, Dur), every drag has a tap way in, Reduce Motion honoured; the automated tells check doesn't scan Kotlin, so it did not run.

**After the run:** failures go on the batch list with the check number; passes move their batch items to "On the device".
