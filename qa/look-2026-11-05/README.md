# The look experiment: physical and flat (2026-11-05)

Every page of Jib on the iPad and the iPhone simulators, in the demo, before and after step 1 of the Skiff Arrange spec (vault `Projects/Dev/fm1-ipad/skiff-arrange-spec.md`).

- **Physical (before):** the app before the change.
- **Physical (after):** the same look after the change, to show it still draws as it did. The only differences are what the demo moves between shots: the playhead, the position readout, and the step the demo edits every 6 seconds.
- **Flat, quiet / medium / strong:** the flat look at each elevation strength (shadow ×0.6 / ×1 / ×1.4, lifted and held surfaces a tone down / as specified / a tone up).

On a device: the connection menu at the top left (iPhone: the ⋯ menu) › Developer › Look and Elevation, in debug builds. As launch arguments: `-lookStyle flat|physical -lookElevation quiet|medium|strong`.

What to look for: the chiclet keys standing on their 2 pt edge, toggles that are on sitting down on it, the active tab and the selected track's chip lifted with a ring, and whether the three strengths read differently enough on the iPad itself. The simulator shots are scaled down to 1600 px wide.
